ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2024

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis. Leave must be approved by the direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days to the following year. Leaves exceeding the carry-forward limit will be forfeited.

1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

1.3 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave. Adoption leave follows the same policy as maternity or paternity leave depending on the adopting parent's gender.

SECTION 2: WORK FROM HOME POLICY

2.1 Eligibility
Work from home (WFH) is available to all employees whose roles can be performed remotely. Employees must have completed their probation period of 6 months before being eligible for WFH arrangements.

2.2 WFH Schedule
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. Core hours of 10am to 4pm must be maintained regardless of work location. Attendance in office is mandatory on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

2.3 Equipment
The company provides a laptop and required software. Home internet and electricity costs are the responsibility of the employee. A one-time home office allowance of INR 10,000 is provided for eligible employees.

SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE AND APPRAISAL

3.1 Appraisal Cycle
Performance appraisals are conducted twice a year: in June and December. Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Below Expectations) to 5 (Exceptional). Ratings of 4 or above are eligible for a performance bonus.

3.2 Salary Increments
Annual salary increments are awarded based on performance ratings. Rating 3 (Meets Expectations): 8% increment. Rating 4 (Exceeds Expectations): 12% increment. Rating 5 (Exceptional): 18% increment plus a special bonus of up to 30% of annual CTC.

3.3 Promotion Policy
Employees are eligible for promotion after a minimum of 2 years in the current role. Promotions require a performance rating of 4 or above in the last two appraisal cycles. All promotions must be approved by the department head and HR.

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT

4.1 Professional Behavior
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism at all times. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any form is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination. All conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR within 5 working days of occurrence.

4.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not share proprietary company information, client data, or internal communications with external parties. Violation of the confidentiality policy may result in legal action.

4.3 Social Media
Employees must not post content that could damage the company's reputation. Personal opinions on social media must be clearly distinguished from official company positions.

SECTION 5: BENEFITS

5.1 Health Insurance
All employees and their immediate family members are covered under a group health insurance plan with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000. The company bears 100% of the premium for the employee. Dependents can be added at the employee's cost.

5.2 Provident Fund
The company contributes 12% of the employee's basic salary to the Provident Fund. This is in addition to the employee's own 12% contribution. PF statements are accessible quarterly through the EPFO portal.

5.3 Gratuity
Employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service are eligible for gratuity as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. Gratuity is calculated as: 15 days salary x years of service.
